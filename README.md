 Project Overview

This project looks at the sales performance of a retail business using historical sales data. The goal was to understand how the business has been performing over time, identify what drives sales and profit, and present the findings in a clear and easy-to-understand dashboard.

The dashboard helps business owners and decision-makers quickly see important trends and make better business decisions.

Project Objectives

* Understand the overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the best-performing product categories.
* Discover the regions and states contributing the most revenue.
* Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Highlight the products that generate the highest sales.

Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides insights into:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Sales Trend by Year
* Monthly Profit Trend
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by State
* Top-Selling Products

Key Insights

* Sales increased consistently over the years, showing steady business growth.
* The highest profits were recorded between September and December, making the end of the year the strongest sales period.
* Technology products generated the highest sales compared to other product categories.
* The West region achieved the highest sales performance.
* California and New York contributed the largest share of profit.
* A small number of products generated a significant portion of the company's revenue.

Recommendations

* Increase marketing and promotional activities during the last quarter of the year to maximize sales.
* Continue investing in the Technology category since it delivers the highest revenue.
* Study the success of the West region and apply similar strategies in other regions.
* Focus on promoting high-performing products while reviewing the performance of lower-selling products.
* Explore opportunities to improve sales in regions with lower performance.

Conclusion

The dashboard shows that the business is growing steadily and remains profitable. By focusing on top-performing products, regions, and seasonal trends, the business can improve sales, increase profit, and make more informed decisions.
